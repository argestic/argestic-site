Support

Questions, a bug, an idea for AVoidX? Write to me at info@argestic.de — in English or German.

What helps me help you

  • Mac or iPhone, and the version of AVoidX (Mac: AVoidX → About AVoidX).
  • The pedal's firmware version.
  • How the pedal is connected: network, USB or Bluetooth.
  • What you did, what you expected, what happened — a screenshot if you can.

Beta on the iPhone

The iPhone app is in beta on TestFlight. If you test it, the TestFlight app's Send Beta Feedback reaches me as well.
