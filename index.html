AVoidX Pedal Controller

An editor and controller for the VoidX StompStation — on the Mac and on the iPhone.

Mac

macOS 14 or later, Apple silicon and Intel.

Download coming soon

iPhone

iOS 18 or later.

Beta on TestFlight

What it does

  • Finds the pedal on your network, over USB or Bluetooth, and keeps in step with it.
  • Presets and the signal chain at a glance: blocks, their settings, models and IRs.
  • A looper with tracks, overdubs and a drum machine that follows your loop.
  • Hands-free: MIDI footswitches step through presets, blocks and the looper.

Free, and private

AVoidX is free. It talks to your pedal and nothing else: no account, no tracking, no data sent to me. The details are in the privacy policy.

AVoidX is an independent app. VoidX and StompStation are names of their respective owners.
